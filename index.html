    How DropLink Works

    DropLink provides two clear transfer modes depending on your needs:

    ⚡ 10 MINUTES (P2P Transfer) Direct WebRTC connection between devices. Fast, zero-server payload, ideal when sender & receiver are online together.
    📅 1 MONTH (Temporary Link) Encrypted persistent storage. Sender can close browser and turn off laptop. Receiver can download anytime within 30 days.

    Security Architecture

    10-Minute transfers use WebRTC DTLS/SRTP E2E encryption without cloud storage. 1-Month transfers use encrypted local persistent storage with strict single-use token deletion.

    Privacy Policy

    No tracking, no central payload logs, and all links expire automatically on download or deadline.
